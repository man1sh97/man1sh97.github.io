Manish Patel

PhD Scholar in Physics
Institute of Physics, Bhubaneswar

I work on stochastic dynamics, active matter, and nonequilibrium statistical physics, with a particular interest in the dynamics and thermodynamics of active particles.

Research

My research focuses on stochastic dynamics and thermodynamics of active matter, with particular interest in active Brownian particles and nonequilibrium systems.
